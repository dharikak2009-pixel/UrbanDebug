About UrbanDebug

UrbanDebug is a civic technology prototype. Think of a city as software: problems like potholes, broken streetlights and overflowing bins are bugs, and UrbanDebug helps find, prioritise and fix them.

1. Report it

Citizens describe a problem with a verified city and street location.

2. Track it

Every report gets a unique ID and moves through Submitted, Under Review, In Progress and Resolved.

3. Fix your city

Administrators see the latest reports, check the AI priority and update the status.

Prototype note: reports are saved in this browser only. A shared database would be the next step.
